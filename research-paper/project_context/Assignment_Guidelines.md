## 2. Core Assignment Rules & Constraints
As communicated in the class lecture and official CR voice/handwritten directives:
1. **Source & Publisher**: Must be a full research paper from a reputable, peer-reviewed journal index (IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect/Elsevier, Springer).
2. **Publication Year**: Must be published **after 2024** (2024, 2025, or 2026).
3. **Paper Length**: Must be a substantial journal paper (~10–15 pages).
4. **Content Requirements**:
   * Critical review of the chosen paper's problem formulation, methodology, and architectural framework.
   * Thorough analysis of experimental results, benchmarks, and baselines.
   * Student group synthesis: what the team learned, strengths/weaknesses, and real-world software engineering application.
5. **Formatting**: Formatted using the standard **IEEE Two-Column Template** (`IEEEtran.cls`).
